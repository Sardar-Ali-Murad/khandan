Our Story

Khandan – A Community Built on Trust and Unity

Khandan began with a simple idea: if one person brings ten more, and they do the same, we can build a family of 100,000 people helping each other. We support small businesses, skill development for children, and help families with essential needs like marriage expenses.

Our Mission

Empower families through unity, trust, and long-term support.

  • Micro Business Support
  • Practical Skills for the Next Generation
